Kielen vaihtaminen - Byta språk - Changing the language

Kielen vaihtaminen: Voit vaihtaa MyCourses-oppimisympäristön käyttöliittymän kielen (suomi, englanti tai ruotsi) profiilivalikosta. Kun olet kirjautunut, klikkaa oikeassa yläkulmassa olevaa profiilikuvaketta ja valitse kieli.

Byta språk: Du kan byta språket i MyCourses-inlärningsmiljön genom att klicka på din profilikon i det övre högra hörnet och välja språk.

Changing the language: You can change the MyCourses learning environment's interface language (Finnish, English, or Swedish) from your profile menu. Once logged in, click your profile icon or your name in the top right corner.

Self enrolment

Click on the "Syllabus" button on the upper right corner of the page. In the view that opens, click on the "Self enrolment" button, and finally the "Enrol me" button. You can read more in the privacy notice.

You can now go through the different themes by scrolling through the headings on the left.

Exchange student at Aalto University

Are you an exchange student coming to Aalto University? Welcome! This course is for new degree students and most of the information is not applicable to you. Please refer instead to the website for exchange students on all matters related to your exchange, including studies and arrival at Aalto. If you have any questions contact the exchange coordinator of your Aalto school.

What are studies like at Aalto? What courses can I take as an exchange student? Studies for incoming exchange students | Aalto University

The goal of the Pre-Orientation

The goal of the Pre-Orientation is to provide the key information about studying at Aalto and the student support services, so that starting your studies goes as smoothly as possible. It serves as preparatory material for orientation week, and you can return to it at any time during the academic year. The material covers topics common to all new Aalto students. Major- and programme-specific practices and instructions are covered during orientation week.
